The Problem

Paper Registers Are Slowing Your Mill Down

Flour mills running on registers face billing delays, ledger disputes, and zero visibility into sales and profit.

Registers Get Damaged & Lost

Paper bills and ledgers tear, stain, and vanish — and every lost page is lost money.

Prices Change, Registers Don't

When flour prices change, old registers keep producing wrong bills and angry customers.

Ledger Disputes at Month-End

Handwritten khata entries get questioned. Without clean records, you absorb the loss.

No Idea What's Actually Selling

Total sales, credit sales, profit — all unknown until someone spends days calculating.
