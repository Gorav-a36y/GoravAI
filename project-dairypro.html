The Problem

Every Dairy Owner Knows This Pain

If you run a dairy shop or milk business, you are managing too many things on paper — and paper always creates problems.

Khata Registers Get Lost

Supplier and customer khata written in registers can be lost, damaged, or disputed. One missing page means lost money.

Month-End Takes Days

Calculating monthly milk ledgers for daily customers by hand is slow, tiring, and full of calculation mistakes.

Waste Is Never Recorded

Broken eggs, spoiled milk, damaged stock — without tracking, you never know how much money waste is eating every month.

No Business Insights

You work hard every day, but you have no clear picture of profit, loss, or which products actually make money.

The Solution

Everything Your Dairy Needs, Built In

DairyPro covers the complete daily cycle of a dairy business — from buying raw material to selling products to closing the month's khata.

Raw Material & Suppliers

Add all raw materials you receive from suppliers — milk, eggs, and more. Create supplier accounts, record every collection, and print the receipt instantly if needed.

Supplier Khata (Ledger)

Not paying a supplier right now? The amount automatically goes to their digital khata. Every pending rupee is recorded — no paper work, no forgotten payments, no disputes.

Create & Sell Products

Make products from your raw materials — yogurt (dahi), desi ghee, and anything else you produce. Sell them with proper pricing and stock tracking.

POS Billing Screen

A simple point-of-sale screen where you select the customer and sell products plus raw materials in seconds. Every sale makes a receipt and updates the khata automatically.

Waste Material Tracking

Eggs broken? Milk spoiled? Add it to the waste page in one entry. You finally see exactly how much loss happens — and where you can control it.

1-Click Daily Milk Entry

For customers who take milk daily and pay monthly — just click one button and the software automatically adds that day's milk to their khata. Month-end collection becomes effortless.

Monthly Ledger Download

Any customer asks for their month's record? Select the customer, choose the month, click download. The full daily milk ledger is ready to print or share on WhatsApp.

Customer Khata

Create customer accounts with their own khata. When someone buys on credit, the receipt and the ledger entry are both created automatically. Full history, always available.

Works On Your Computer

The desktop version runs fully on your own computer — no internet needed for daily billing and khata. Your data stays with you, safe on your machine.
