The Problem

Running a Pharmacy on Paper Is Costing You Money

Stockouts, expired medicines, billing delays, and zero financial visibility — the daily reality of pharmacies without a proper system.

No Inventory Control

You discover stockouts when a customer asks — and overstocked items quietly block your cash.

Expired Stock Losses

Without expiry tracking, expired medicines sit on shelves until they become pure loss.

Slow Manual Billing

Handwritten receipts slow the queue and make errors that are impossible to trace later.

No Financial Visibility

Sales, margins, and profit exist only in your head — and tax time becomes a paperwork crisis.

The Solution

Everything Your Pharmacy Needs in One System

From stock room to checkout counter to your accountant's desk — one connected platform.

Medicine Inventory Management

Full stock control with quantities, categories, and reorder alerts so you never face an empty shelf at the wrong moment.

Expiry Date Tracking

Every batch tracked with expiry alerts. Sell near-expiry stock in time and stop throwing money in the bin.

Fast POS Billing

Scan, bill, print — quick checkout even at peak hours. Every sale updates stock and accounts automatically.

Supplier Accounts & Ledger

Manage suppliers, purchases, and pending payments in one clean digital ledger. No more forgotten invoices.

Prescription Records

Keep patient prescription history attached to customer profiles for fast, safe refills.

Business Reports

Sales, profit, stock movement, and top-selling medicines — clear reports whenever you need them.

AI WhatsApp Assistant for Customers

A multilingual AI assistant answers customer queries 24/7 — medicine availability, opening hours, and order questions — in their own language.

AI Accountant for the Owner

Ask about profit, margins, or slow-moving stock in plain words. The AI analyzes your entire business and answers like a chartered accountant on your payroll.
